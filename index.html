FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about NeatRow Exports.

How does 1 row per order work for multi-item orders?

NeatRow aggregates line item SKUs, titles, and quantities into pipe-delimited columns (e.g. SKU-A | SKU-B). This ensures order total prices and taxes never duplicate, making spreadsheet sums and accounting formulas 100% accurate.

Will special characters and SKUs open properly in Microsoft Excel?

Yes. NeatRow Exports includes a UTF-8 Byte Order Mark (BOM) on all exported files, preventing Excel from garbling foreign accents, currency symbols, and alphanumeric SKUs.

Do you store customer personal information (PII)?

No. NeatRow Exports requests strictly the read_orders scope and does not access or store customer names, emails, or phone numbers. Customer ID and email columns are exported as blank strings.

How long are export files kept?

Export CSV files are stored in Cloudflare R2 and are automatically deleted after 14 days. Metadata (run history row counts and timestamps) is kept until you delete the template or uninstall the app.

What happens if I uninstall the app?

Upon uninstall, store sessions are deleted immediately. All remaining store data and templates are deleted within 48 hours in compliance with Shopify's mandatory GDPR shop/redact webhook.
